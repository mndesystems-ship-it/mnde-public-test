Decide what your AI agents are allowed to do

Author rules in plain intent — allow a tool, require human approval, or refuse it outright. MNDe compiles them to a signed, enforceable policy. Anything you don't explicitly allow is refused by default.

Human review

For each capability the agent exposes, choose Allow, Ask me first, or Block. MNDe's recommendation is shown, but you decide — and nothing is authorized until you activate the compiled policy.

Effective authority

Generated from the compiled policy — exactly what the engine will enforce.
